# Week 13 — Candidate-facing demo script
**Presenter:** Hana Dager Abascal · **Approximate duration:** 2–3 minutes. Only use a verified READY preview URL. Never demo unreleased experience features on production.

## Screen 1 — Why we changed direction (0:00–0:25)
**SHOW** unchanged production `https://proofofskill.app` for context, then switch to new Week 13 preview homepage.
**SAY:** “Week 12 asked candidates to perform a bounded finance exercise. Five qualitative response records raised a different concern: candidates often already had relevant projects and operational work, but the exercise was not the same as describing those experiences. These are reported concerns, not measured usability results.”

## Screen 2 — Two starting options (0:25–0:45)
**SHOW** redesigned Week 13 homepage.
**SAY:** “Week 13 now gives people a choice: describe existing experience or try the original financial exercise. The original is still there; no extra assessment is required before telling your story.”

## Screen 3 — Fill experience without private data (0:45–1:25)
**SHOW** “Describe my existing experience”. Select **Family business** or **Academic project**, switch to Spanish, and use synthetic demonstrator words:
- What I did: “I organized supplier invoices and tracked payments that required follow-up.”
- Analysis: “I compared purchase options and checked recurring payment needs.”
- Evidence: “I can explain the method without private supplier figures.”
- Limits: “I do not claim verified cost savings or formal approval authority.”
**SAY:** “These are synthetic demonstration statements. The system structures my own words; it does not invent performance metrics, titles or employer endorsements. It warns against sharing confidential files.”

## Screen 4 — Review, edit and reuse (1:25–2:05)
**SHOW** generate summary, edit résumé-ready statement, click Copy and save a `.txt` file.
**SAY:** “Candidates control the wording before sharing. The output is explicitly candidate-reported and not independently verified. They can copy or download it without signing in. This new path does not upload their experience to Supabase; the original Google sign-in and private finance save remain unchanged.”

## Screen 5 — Evidence and what we still do not know (2:05–2:35)
**SHOW** financial exercise route and GitHub tests.
**SAY:** “Automated tests check basic validations and claim boundaries; synthetic personas check three fictional backgrounds. This is NOT proof of faster applications, actual repeat use, or hiring impact. The response records do not establish three verified strangers or observed timings. A genuine before/after test with consent is still necessary.”

## Close (2:35–2:50)
**SAY:** “The visible Week 13 change is that candidates can finally explain what they already did. Whether that is better than Word, LinkedIn or general AI must be determined through real, independent comparative use. Production is unchanged until approval.”
