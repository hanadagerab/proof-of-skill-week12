# Week 15 — Verified background sources and Mexican adaptation (21 Nov 2026)
**Scope:** External context for a fictional, educational distribution demonstration. These sources do NOT validate Proof-of-Skill's actual performance, demand, hiring fairness or effects.

| Source (official/public) | Verified contextual mechanism | What cannot be inferred |
|---|---|---|
| INEGI, Censos Económicos 2024, definitive results, https://www.inegi.org.mx/programas/ce/2024/default.html | Nationwide establishment and sector data, including micro/small/medium establishments. Useful for explaining the Mexican SME setting. | Counts do not establish willingness to pay for this product or frequency of a specific hiring problem. |
| INEGI, ENDUTIH 2025, https://www.inegi.org.mx/programas/endutih/2025/default.html | Household/person digital access survey; relevant to designing low-friction and alternative non-digital pathways. | Internet use is not proof all candidates can or want to use digital hiring evidence. |
| Harvard/Burning Glass, Skills-Based Hiring: The Long Road from Pronouncements to Practice, https://pw.hks.harvard.edu/post/skills-based-hiring-the-long-road-from-pronouncements-to-practice | Report describes the gap between degree-requirement announcements and changed hiring practices. | US findings are not Mexican product-market fit or proof skills-first practices automatically remove exclusion. |
| TestGorilla, https://www.testgorilla.com/skills-tests/ | Provides structured job-related skills tests and assessment workflows; compare as existing option, not an inferior straw man. | Does not prove employer need for an additional candidate task. |
| Applied, https://www.beapplied.com/ | Recruiting system with skills/structured hiring mechanisms; benchmark human screening workflows. | No claim its functions are absent or that Proof-of-Skill outperforms it. |
| LinkedIn skills-first research, https://pulse.linkedin.com/research-and-trends/publications/skills-first-report | Analysis of framing skills vs job title for labor-market opportunity. | Does not validate any claimed causal improvement from Proof-of-Skill. |

**Implications for Mexican localization:**
- Spanish-first, concrete spreadsheet delivery example and clear, mobile-readable labels; content aimed at SME human evaluators.
- Voluntary existing samples, no duplicate required assessment; candidate correction and equivalent consideration must be proven operationally before any employer adoption claim.
- Employer-facing pilot price of MXN 2,500 and estimated MXN 800 contribution are historical modeling assumptions, not external benchmarks or paid demand.
- Proposed distribution method: consent-first one-to-one employer outreach with honest feedback ledger, not public likes as a stand-in for usefulness.

**Research audit:** The three content assets demonstrate logical distinctions, not external numerical claims. Fictional 12-order example was authored for the walkthrough: 10 on-time / 12 total = 83.3%; two delays. Neither INEGI nor competitors are evidence that this fictional case reflects real candidates.
