# Proof-of-Skill — Product Charter (pre-code, v1.0)

**Author:** Hana Dager Abascal. **Status:** planned hypothesis, not validated market adoption.

## Vision
In three years, Proof-of-Skill could allow candidates whose credentials are not conventional to have relevant analytical work meaningfully considered, with transparent limits to what any artifact proves. Evaluators remain accountable for hiring decisions. Value depends on demonstrably replacing or shortening—not duplicating—existing screening work and measuring actual candidate access.

## Users and payer hypothesis
Candidate: applicant for entry-level analytical roles with practical skills and limited conventional credentials. Evaluator: hiring manager or trained reviewer authorized to inspect a bounded analyst work sample. Payer: employer or recruitment team, **unvalidated hypothesis**; candidate should not be charged or subjected to a mandatory duplicate test.

## Principles
1. Never elevate submitted work into verified authorship or employability.
2. Make observed, inferred, missing and unverified explicit.
3. Preserve human authority and allow clarification/stop.
4. Minimize candidate time, device and accessibility burden.
5. Default to privacy and least privilege.

## Non-goals
Automated hiring/rejection/ranking; identity or AI-authorship detection; guaranteed employment; whole-person skill certification; mandatory second assessment; 10x real-world claim; bulk ATS integration; payment or marketplace.

## North Star
Share of candidates previously not substantively assessed through conventional credentials whose relevant submitted work receives a **documented substantive human consideration**, without adding mandatory duplicate screening. Not measurable until a real pilot and operational definition are established.

## Core Concepts
**Task:** bounded analyst exercise. **Criterion:** evidence requirement. **Submission:** user-provided structured task response. **Disclosure:** statement of assistance and authorship, not verification. **Evidence item:** criterion-linked classification. **Boundary:** permissible and prohibited conclusions. **Summary:** bounded human-readable output. **Decision:** evaluator-owned next action.

## Exact Method engine (Blueprint)
“Step 4 of my Method becomes the function that converts task submissions into claim-bounded evidence summaries, distinguishing what the submitted work supports, what remains unresolved about authorship or performance, and which judgments belong to human evaluators.”

## Six Blueprint conditions
1. Real affected person required before demand claim (not yet established). 2. Encode evidence boundaries (assigned). 3. Compare complete work against current employer method. 4. Assign responsible actors and external dependencies. 5. Human decision stop/escalation. 6. Shadow Clause: simulated, predicted, reported and intermediate results are never verified real-world success.

## Concession and falsification
Do not add duplicate mandatory screening or make unsupported employability claims. Interview five real entry-level hiring decision-makers. If none would materially replace or shorten an existing assessment, reconsider the venture. No real interview is yet evidenced.
