## Before each 30–45 minute session
1. Privately prepare a blank [SESSION_TEMPLATE.md](SESSION_TEMPLATE.md) with ID, date, relationship, screen size and local time. Do not upload identifiable research notes to public GitHub.
2. Have a timer and a private de-identified note sheet; recording only with **separate** recording consent. Check whether participant can reach the frozen preview. Preserve any access failure as observed friction.
3. Ask the participant to avoid employer documents, customer information, usernames, passwords, screenshots containing personal details, or production credentials. They may invent identifying details while keeping the work episode truthful; distinguish truly fictional data.
4. Read [CONSENT.md](CONSENT.md). Obtain consent for participation and separately for **exact quotations**, optional recording, and optional follow-up. If refused, thank them and stop; log refusal privately. If consent withdrawn, stop.
5. Do not demonstrate the app or explain its financial exercise before observing the existing workaround.

## Exact timed session script (30–45 minutes)
| Time | Moderator: say exactly | Participant: do | Observer: record |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–4 min | “Thank you. I am observing how people describe their real analytical work when applying to jobs. This is not a job interview or an assessment. There are no right answers, and you may stop or skip any part. We will look at your usual method first. Please use a de-identified example.” | Consent, choose device/language | Consent status, stranger category, work context |
| 4–6 min | “Think of one real occasion when you organized information, compared options, improved a process, or analyzed numbers. What was your role? What can you describe without sharing confidential information?” | Select one genuine episode | Activity type; ownership, assistance, disclosure limitations |
| 6–14 min | **“Using the tools you normally use, show how you would describe that experience for a relevant job application. Work as you normally would. Tell me when you consider it usable.”** | Build/update résumé bullet or explanation using preferred tool | Stopwatch, navigation, steps, rework, questions, final artifact, ability to explain |
| 14–24 min | **“Now open this version of Proof-of-Skill and try to communicate that same experience. Work without my guidance; stopping is okay. Tell me when you have something you would actually use.”** | Independently explore frozen preview; may find fixed exercise irrelevant | Stopwatch including navigation, non-completion, sign-in friction, irrelevant steps, errors, confidence, privacy, output and accuracy. Avoid helping except for safety |
| 24–32 min | **“You can now use ordinary résumé guidance or a general-purpose AI tool to express the same real experience. Use whichever alternative you think is credible. If using AI, do not provide identifying or confidential information.”** | Produce an alternative result | Prompt exactly if consented/de-identified, time, manual corrections, factual overstatements, candidate comprehension |
| 32–40 min | “Which result, if any, would you actually use in an application, and why? Which parts felt unnecessary? Did any statement claim more than you can support? How would you improve it? Is there a real reason you would return to Proof-of-Skill without being asked?” | Answer neutrally | Exact quotes **only with permission**, use intention vs actual behavior, criticism and other tools preferred |
| 40–45 min | “Thank you. Is there any part of your notes or outputs you would like me not to use? Would you be willing to be contacted for a later comparison? Saying no is completely fine.” | Optional follow-up choice | Withdrawal, retention, permission, no-pressure close |

**Moderation rules:** No product pitching; no “Wasn't that easy?”; no leading a participant to a specific button. If asked for help, respond once: “What would you try next?” and log the prompt. If safety is at risk, intervene and record the intervention. Mark a task as **assisted**, not independently completed. Never ask a candidate to fabricate employment achievements or submit confidential evidence. A refusal or a comparison that favors ordinary AI is valuable evidence.

## Record while observing, not from memory afterward
- Time start/end for **usual method**, **Proof-of-Skill**, **credible alternative**; include login, navigation, correction, copying, and refusal time as applicable.
- Count steps and rework, unclear labels, irrelevant finance questions, help requests, errors and unsupported statements; distinguish participant mistakes from interface failures.
- Capture a de-identified output from each method **only if permitted**, then ask candidate to verify factual accuracy. Do not treat attestation as independent employer verification.
- Record result: independently completed / partially completed / failed / declined / blocked; usefulness 1–5; effort 1–5; comprehension 1–5; voluntary preferred output; authentic return **intent** separately from observed return.
- Three-column note-taking: (1) **timestamped action**, (2) **exact words** with quote consent, (3) **researcher's hypothesis**. Never put a paraphrase in quotation marks.
- At close, privately write a five-line factual summary and 1–3 pain candidates; do **not** rank the final three until all five genuine sessions have been reviewed.
