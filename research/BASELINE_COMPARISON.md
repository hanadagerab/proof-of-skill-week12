# Baseline comparison protocol — Week 13
**State: protocol only; no real-user measurements yet.**

## Research question
For the **same real job-relevant analytical activity**, does Proof-of-Skill yield a more useful, defensible statement of capability than the candidate's usual process or competent ordinary résumé/general-purpose AI assistance, without adding harmful work?

## Within-participant comparison
1. Observe candidate's own workflow first, without mentioning suggested tools. Freeze a de-identified account of the exact work episode.
2. Candidate independently tries existing Proof-of-Skill (current fixed financial task), and may decline because it is irrelevant.
3. Offer a fair alternative: their usual résumé-support approach or a well-instructed general-purpose AI prompt using the same non-confidential episode. Preserve prompt text and disclosures. Rotate the order of Proof-of-Skill versus alternative across participants where practical, but never place a tool before the first natural-workaround observation.
4. Do not invent a skill demonstration if the participant cannot safely disclose work. They may use a fictional exercise labeled fictional.
5. Evaluate candidate burden end-to-end, including sign-in, copying, correcting, exporting, and any duplicated assessment.

## Measures (predefine, not retro-fit)
| Metric | Operational definition | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Time | Stopwatch from start prompt to candidate-declared usable final description (seconds), including sign-in | Observer |
| Effort | Count deliberate actions, rework cycles and extra assessments; 1–5 perceived burden | Observation + post task |
| Independent completion | Usable truthful statement without coaching: yes / partial / no / refused | Observer and participant |
| Errors | Count inaccurate claims, misleading verification, math mistakes and omissions separately | Candidate review + observer |
| Clarity | Candidate can explain meaning and boundaries of summary; 1–5 anchored question | Candidate explanation |
| Truthfulness | Candidate explicitly confirms whether each factual statement matches their work; unknown remains unknown | Candidate attestation, not employer verification |
| Privacy | Number of confidentiality requests or risky disclosures, and whether candidate can stop | Observation |
| Voluntary intention/action | Specific real-use example, follow-up action, and separately verified return | Unprompted report or observed event |

## Comparison fairness and reporting
Use equivalent content and a neutral script. Give each method the same opportunity to clarify its output; log unequal exposure, order and facilitator intervention. Report raw per-user times and outcomes plus median/range for n=5; do not imply significance, causal hiring impact or 10× advantage. Show negative and mixed cases. Keep simulated Week 4 49.2 to 17.5 minutes (~2.8×) separate and visibly labeled SIMULATED.
