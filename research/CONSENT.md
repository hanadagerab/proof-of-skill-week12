# Participant consent — Week 13 Proof-of-Skill
**Researcher:** Hana Dager Abascal | **Purpose:** Compare existing job-application methods against Proof-of-Skill for communicating practical analytical experience. This is academic product research, not recruitment.

## Plain-language statement to read before the session
"You are invited to test how you usually explain skills in an application and to try a research prototype. Participation is voluntary. You can stop, refuse an activity, avoid recording, or ask that your identifiable notes not be used without losing anything. You will not receive a job opportunity, employment recommendation, or verified skills certificate. Please use invented or de-identified examples: no customer records, employer spreadsheets, financial secrets, IDs or passwords. The tool may ask you to use Google sign-in to test saving; that step is optional and can be skipped. Do not share login credentials or allow me to see your password. I will measure time, confusion, task completion and output clarity, and may capture your exact words only with permission. De-identified findings may appear in a university submission, but your identifying records will not be posted publicly."

## Handling and withdrawal
Research notes with contact info or consent originals: restricted, separately stored; only anonymized aggregated findings are eligible for public commits. Obtain permission before screenshots and blur/remove identifiers. Retain only as long as needed for course submission under a stated retention plan; participant may request removal of researcher-held identifiable research data before aggregation, subject to stated limits. Do **not** assert that deleting an app account or saved evidence is available unless tested; current MVP appears not to grant delete. Never promise control over an exported shared copy.
