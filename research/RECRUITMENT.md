# Week 13 — Recruitment plan | Proof-of-Skill
Owner: Hana Dager Abascal · Role: USER · Status: planned, zero interviews recorded.

## Sample and independent recruitment
Recruit **five real candidates** with hands-on analytical experience (e.g., inventory, supplier comparisons, sales or cost tracking), limited polished portfolio or conventional credential signals, and a recent or active application to an entry-level analytical/operations role. Recruit **at least three strangers** with no existing close friendship/family relationship or interest in pleasing the founder. Record relationship category as stranger, acquaintance or close relation before each session. Two participants may be acquaintances, but not substitutes for the stranger minimum. Avoid recruiting only candidates who already like the product. Prioritize candidates from different educational routes, Spanish-first speakers and mobile/low-bandwidth use where feasible. Do not exclude informal experience.

## Invitation, verbatim
"I am researching how people describe practical analytical work when applying for jobs in Mexico City. This is not a hiring process or skills assessment, and there is no promise of a job. I would like to observe your usual application approach first, then compare a prototype. The session is voluntary, about 30–45 minutes, and you may skip any question. Please do not bring employer-confidential documents. Would you like the consent information?"

## Screening (do not collect unnecessary identifiers)
- Pseudonym / ID: U01–U05; recruitment channel and relationship; consent status
- Recent example of analytical activity? (self-report, not verified skill)
- Applied or likely to apply for an entry-level relevant job? When?
- Usual device and preferred language; accessibility needs volunteered
- Strangers: independent of researcher and venture? yes/no; evidence of recruitment method, not identity publication

## Scheduling and refusals
Recruit more than five only if necessary; log every invitation, unanswered approach, refusal and no-show in a restricted/private researcher log; never list names or contact details in public GitHub. Declines do not count as completed sessions. No pressure or incentives conditioned on favorable feedback. For five completed sessions verify at least three stranger relationships. Do not recruit employer subordinates under coercion.

## Gate
No claims of completed recruitment, findings, or product demand until genuine dated session evidence exists. Team 5 collective 'Agua en seguimiento' is separate from this candidate study. The Blueprint is still marked simulated/pending actual team approval.
