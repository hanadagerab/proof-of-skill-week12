1. **Use work already performed:** U01, U02, U03 and U04 describe a mismatch or the need to enter existing academic/operational/informal work rather than solve a separate financial task. The finance-experienced U02 still values bounded exercises as **different** evidence.
2. **Time-to-usable-output / added burden:** U01 requests a short editable statement; U03 says an unrelated exercise is extra work; U05 questions the practical value of another document. There are no measured task times, steps, abandonment rates or completion rates.
3. **Truthful provenance and accessibility:** U02/U05 distinguish self-reported experience, platform work sample and employer confirmation; U04 requires clear Spanish-language explanation and privacy rules. U01/U03 also oppose invented savings or unrelated claims.
4. **Existing alternatives are credible:** Each record identifies an existing résumé or AI workflow that the candidate may prefer. None evidences automatic replacement of those alternatives or demand for a paid product.
